# SEO governance

## Goal

Build a strong feeder-site and issue/entity content network without crossing into scaled low-value spam, doorway behavior, or fake commentary.

## Rules

- Every feeder page must have a distinct audience, city, issue, or contrast rationale.
- Do not publish near-duplicate pages whose primary purpose is ranking manipulation.
- External placement is human-operated only.
- Do not automate comments, forum posts, or fake personas.
- Use campaign disclosures where required.
- Prefer source-backed synthesis over scraped or lightly rewritten articles.
- Link back only when relevant to the discussion and editorial context.

## Preferred content rails

- Local city + issue explainers
- Candidate accomplishment / credibility pages
- Contrast pages grounded in public record
- News-response pages connecting outside developments to TX-24
- Turnout and action pages tied to election timing

## Avoid

- Mass-produced doorway pages
- Generic AI sludge with swapped city names
- Copy-paste comment campaigns
- Misleading affiliation or undisclosed advocacy
- Thin pages that exist only to funnel to the main site
